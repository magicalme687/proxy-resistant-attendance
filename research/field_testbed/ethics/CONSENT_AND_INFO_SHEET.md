## What the study is
We are testing an attendance app that checks three things: that you can see the classroom screen (a rotating QR code), that your phone is near the classroom Bluetooth beacon, and that the selfie matches your enrolled face. We want to measure how often it works for genuine students, how long it takes, and whether volunteers acting as "attackers" can fool it.

## What you will do
- Install the test app on your phone (or use a lab phone).
- Enrol one face photo.
- Mark attendance as usual in up to __ sessions over __ weeks (about 5 seconds each time).
- Optionally: take part in supervised "attack" trials (e.g. trying to mark for a friend). These are role-play only and have **no** effect on your real attendance record.
- Fill in a 10-question usability questionnaire (SUS), about 3 minutes.

## What we collect
| Data | Kept as | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Face template (512 numbers, not the photo) | Encrypted on the study server | Deleted at study end (__/__/____) |
| Selfie images | **Not stored**: processed in memory and discarded | — |
| Bluetooth signal strength, timestamps, pass/fail per check | Linked to a random code, not your name | Kept for 5 years for research integrity, anonymised |
| Phone model and OS version | Linked to the random code | As above |
| Questionnaire answers | Random code | As above |

Your name and enrolment number are kept separately from the study data, only to send reminders, and are deleted at study end. Published results are aggregate only.

## Your rights
Participation is voluntary and **does not affect your grades or official attendance**. You can withdraw at any time without giving a reason, and ask for your data to be deleted, by contacting the PI. This study follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India).

## Risks and benefits
Risks are minimal (a few seconds of your time per session; a small amount of battery use). There is no direct benefit to you. The results may help build fairer, proxy-resistant attendance systems.
